Projects

  • Plenum Theory: On the Origin of Spacetime

    Independent theoretical physics research proposing a unified algebraic framework for spacetime, the Standard Model, and cosmological evolution from a single postulate. Across 55 zero-parameter theorems, it derives four-dimensional spacetime (d = 4), three fermion generations, Standard Model gauge structure and mixing hierarchies, inflationary observables matching Planck 2018 to 0.35σ, and dynamical dark energy matching DESI DR2 (2025) to 0.15σ.

  • Meshless Radial Point Interpolation for Self-Consistent Maxwell–Schrödinger Modelling of Nanoscale Devices

    Presented at the Science Undergraduate Research Conference 2026 (Azim Premji University), this work formulates a meshless radial point interpolation method (RPIM) for self-consistent Maxwell–Schrödinger modelling of classical electromagnetic fields and quantum systems in nanoscale devices. Building on a semiclassical FDTD framework (potential formulation, Lorenz gauge, PML boundaries), it integrates quantum algorithms on IBM’s quantum infrastructure with AI/ML for quantum state analysis, simulation optimization, and computational microwave imaging.

  • Starbase

    A centralized material selection and recommendation engine for engineering and research, drawing on 6.1 million source-traced records across metals, polymers, ceramics, and composites. Replacing manual handbook searches, Starbase queries application requirements—operating conditions, load, temperature range, corrosion resistance, weight, manufacturing process, and budget—to return optimal materials with full property justifications and original sources.

  • Celestium

    My vision for a next-generation aerospace company advancing electric and hybrid-electric vertical flight through the Modular Aerial Lift Utility System (MALUS). Within it, Taraka X-1 is a medical utility aircraft engineered with the patient at the heart of its design for rapid emergency extraction and uninterrupted in-transit critical care.

  • Mars Rover Dust Mitigation

    An ongoing mission study assigned by the Vikram Sarabhai Space Centre (VSSC) under Prof. Murali Krishnan K H and Dr. Sreeja S. Engineers dust-mitigation systems for the solar panels of ISRO’s proposed Mangalyaan-2 rover, safeguarding continuous power generation across long-duration surface operations in the Martian dust environment.

  • Next-generation Environment for Human Advancement (NEHA)

    NEHA is the intelligence behind Vespera, working beside the physician in the consulting room as its reach extends from a single patient to the health of a whole population. Built on a three-pillar conceptual design, its research is aimed at using biotechnology to power it with artificial brain cells—driven by a vision of interfacing with the human brain to resolve human bandwidth limits and unleash maximum cognitive capacities.

  • Vespera

    An AI-driven healthcare orchestration platform powered by NEHA uniting the entire continuum of care. For patients, it integrates appointment scheduling, predictive hospital availability, dynamic queue tracking, cab and ambulance logistics, prescription and lab integration, home consultations, medicine delivery, real-time care navigation, and longitudinal medical records via privacy-preserving Universal and Temporary Vespera IDs with time-limited clinical sharing. For clinicians and public health, it delivers ambient consultation capture for auto-generated documentation, one-action “Magic Button” automation, real-time “Magic Whispers” multimodal decision support, Digital Twin pre-intervention testing, Time Machine longitudinal outcome forecasting, HoloPatient mixed-reality procedure validation with quantified risk prediction, and anonymized population-scale early-warning analytics.

  • Spiritus

  • Coastal Adviser
